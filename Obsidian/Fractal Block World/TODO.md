# Trophy

## I2

[[I2 WIG Flower]]/[[I3 WIG Flower]]
> There is a trophy in the stem of WIG Flowers. If you are inside an I2 or I3 WIG Flower and attempt to leave using Blue Rings, you will be caught in the stem of the WIG Flower. 

 [[Toronto]]
 > There is a trophy by the main waypoint in each Toronto City.
 
 [[Toronto Maze]]
 > In the Underground of a Toronto City there is a maze. In the Map Room of such a maze, there is a trophy.
 
 [[Small Blue Flower]]
 > There is a trophy in the treasure room of the secondary part of each Small Blue Flower. There is a 50% chance you will find a secondary part inside the treasure room of the primary part.
 
 [[Willow Tree]]
 > There is a trophy in the center of Willow Trees. These are large pink trees that appear sometimes on Fanatic Islands.
 
 [[Dark Willow Tree]]
 > There is a trophy in the center of Dark Willow Trees. You can sometimes find these on the top of Fanatic Islands. You can also find one with a 50% chance at the center of Weed Rings.

[[Mystic Vines]]
 > There is a trophy in the central shaft of Mystic Vines. You can find these hanging from (normal) Willow Trees.

[[Blue Mushroom]]
> There is a trophy in the treasure room of each Blue Mushroom. You can learn where to find Blue Mushrooms in the I2 Library.

[[Fanatic Spire]]
> You can find a trophy in the treasure room of each Fanatic Spire, which can be found inside Fanatic Lattices and Violets.

[[Creeping Jeni Plant]]
> You can find a trophy in the treasure room of each Creeping Jeni Plant, which can be found inside Fanatic Spires.

[[Violet]]
> There is a trophy inside Violets.
>
> You can find these inside Creeping Jeni Plants and sometimes on the ground of the Minigun Ammo Forest.

[[I2 Mountain]]
> There is a trophy inside I2 Mountains.
> 
> These can be found in Ostrich Ferns (which can be found in Violets and Bubble Caves).
## I3

> The trophy list room for I3 (of the Infinity Flower) trophies can be found in [[Colchester]] Towns.

## Burlington

> The trophy list room for [[Burlington]] can be found at the top of the central tower of each Burlington City.

## Space

> The trophy list room for Space trophies can be found in the [[Space Library]]. This library is a Moon, and can be found around certain planets (and those planets are around suns, and those suns are in galaxies).

## Mylantis

> The trophy list room for Mylantis trophies can be found in the Top City of Mylantis (in the underground).
