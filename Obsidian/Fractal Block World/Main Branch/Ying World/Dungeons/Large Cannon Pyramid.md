> 	1) 5 Cannon Num Shots upgrades,

>
> You can find an Upgrade Station next to a waypoint in the Secret Farmhouse.
>
> The Secret Farmhouse can be found at the end of the Burlington City Combination Lock. This is a very advanced secret.
