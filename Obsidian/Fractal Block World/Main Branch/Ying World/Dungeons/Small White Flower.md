Found inside [[Ying Flower]]
Can also be found under the starting island that has the tutorial


> A small white flower is challenging but is very useful for
> 	1) getting health upgrades,
> 	2) getting ivory armor (600%), and
> 	3) getting nukes. 

Entrance
> This is a Small White Flower. This room is the only way in and out. 
> 
> After this point, there are two ways to the treasure. 
>
> The first way is the front door: go in guns blazing. Go to the bottom of the shaft, then go back up to the top and look for a green platform.
>
> The second way is the back door which requires going through a 6x6x6 maze (but has easier monsters). You get additional treasure the second way. 
>
>The choice is yours. 

Main Treasure Room
> Message from your friend [[K]]:
>
> What is Inner Botany Theory? That is one of the first questions I asked myself when I entered this place.
> 
> On the surface it is the study of inner space. For example the classification of flowers based on what each flower contains in its core. A [[Jumbo Yellow Flower]] contains 5 Small Yellow Flowers in its core, etc.
 >
> But delving a bit deeper, Inner Botany Theory is concerned with objects called Inner Cores which allegedly exist deep inside inner space.
>
> The main question is: Are [[Inner Core]]s necessary for existence?
>
> Evidence for an affirmative answer to this is that universes created in labs are always eventually confirmed to contain Inner Cores: a painstaking verification carried out by generations of Inner Botanists.
