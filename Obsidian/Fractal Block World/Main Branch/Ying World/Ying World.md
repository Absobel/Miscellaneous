Good place to find [[Small Yellow Flower]] and [[Small Cannon Pyramid]] (on islands of the Ying World)

> A [[Ying World]] exists within a [[Ying Flower]]

Has islands surrounded by big electric fences : has [[Ying World Gated Community]] in them

> If you keep shrinking going deeper and deeper into the bottom of a [[Ying World]] forest, you will find interesting things.

Two ways to leave (from [[Ying Library]]):
> One way to leave the [[Ying World]] is as follows: become large and fly through the the world, going past many islands, until you reach either the front, back, left, or right wall.  You can go through the (black) wall by going through tunnels. You will find yourself in a large room with a yin-yang pattern on the walls. Exit that room and the 3 containing rooms.
>
> Note: in one of the shell rooms you can find a green island, which contains a list and the locations of all Ying World Trophies. - [[TODO]]

>There is another way to leave the Ying World, but it is time consuming!
>
> Go to the gray lattice which surrounds the islands of the Ying World. If you shrink and fly through the gray bars of the lattice, you will eventually find an island. That island has a 7x7x7 maze inside it.
>
> If you get through the maze, you will find a Pink Ring Device which will take you outside the Ying World. 
>
> The island also has a "[[Arcade|Minigun Tower]]" on it!
