> The treasure room of the sewers of a Richmond City contains a secret message.

> In a [[Richmond city]], buildings with a question mark at the top have a map of the [[Tau Caves]]. However, the map is incomplete! 
>
> The map says that [[Type 3 Tau Caves]] have paper and cardboard boxes. What the map does NOT say is that Type 3 Tau Caves also have blue cubes ([[Basic Laser Mazes]]). Their treasure room is worth visiting at least once. 
> 
> Specifically, I think their treasure room describes a slight shortcut down the [[Main Branch|main branch]] that will allow you to skip an "[[Orange]]".
