> Remember this: in the [[Ying World]], below the starting island that has the tutorial, there is a [[Small White Flower|small WHITE flower]]. 
> 
> Hence, every [[Ying Flower|YING flower]] has a small WHITE flower inside itself.
> 
> A small white flower is challenging but is very useful for
> 	1) getting health upgrades,
> 	2) getting ivory armor (600%), and
> 	3) getting nukes. 

> There are caves inside this Ying Cave Island. In the first layer of detail, you can find yellow treasure rooms called "[[Layer 1 Treasure Rooms]]".
> 
> There is a chance that one of these will allow you to shrink, and if you shrink one more time (and go through the "pipe layer") you can enter red "[[Layer 2 Treasure Rooms]]".
>
> You can explore the caves outside these treasure rooms to find yellow "[[Layer 3 Treasure Rooms]]", which are special because there you can get upgrades for your Laser weapon.
>
> Finally, the green cubes you can find on that layer are actually "[[Layer 4 Treasure rooms]]", which are special because there you can get Health and Minigun upgrades.
