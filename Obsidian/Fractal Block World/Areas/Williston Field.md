> In Williston Fields, you can find EMP Castles. 
>
> These have EMP upgrades, and also a map room.

> You have probably noticed that when you are larger than a Williston Field, to enter it you can go down a tube which allows you to shrink. There are four types of tubes:
>
> Short tubes: These allow you to shrink directly. 
>
> Tubes with a white top: These give you health upgrades at the end.
>
> Tubes with a green top: These give you a Minigun Max Ammo upgrade!
>
> Red tubes: These allow you to enter AND EXIT.
