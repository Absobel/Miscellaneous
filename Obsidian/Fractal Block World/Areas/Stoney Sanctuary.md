Found in [[Yellow Cube]]

> This is a Stoney Sanctuary. 
>
> There are 4 Rocket upgrades and 2 Health upgrades. 
>
> There is a waypoint right next to this text box (once you shrink).
>
> Once you shrink here, you cannot enlarge. 

Inside
> This is a Stoney Sanctuary, which is part of the [[Main Branch]]. The Main Branch is the canonical path to the depths of inner space which contain [[Inner Core]]s.
>
> To continue down the main branch, go outside this sanctuary into the air that surrounds with black cubes.  Shrink and go to the sides of such a black cube and find an [[Orange]].

> Make sure to visit half way up the tower of our sanctuary.
>
> We have an important message kept there.

> Some places to find Rocket upgrades:
>
> 1) [[Yellow Room]]s inside [[Blue Bubble]]s (various upgrades)
> 2) [[Yellow Bee Cube]]s inside [[Mossy Air]] (3 Damage)
> 3) [[Jericho]], inside a [[Mossy Island]] (various upgrades)
> 4) Stoney Sanctuaries (4 types)
> 5) Top of [[Apple Tree|apple trees]] (2 Max Ammo)
> 6) Yellow Cubes in an Orange [[Outer Mantle]] (Velocity)
> 7) Top of buildings in [[Williston]] (Velocity)
> 8) [[Blackberry|Blackberries]] (6 radius)

> Going to a Stoney Sanctuary such as this should not be a chore. A smart idea is to go to one of these sanctuaries while you are on the way to something else.
>
> For example, one way to exit Group #2 of the [[Infinity Flower]] ([[I2]]) is to shrink into [[Stoney Air]], enter a [[Yellow Cube]], and then go the waypoint next to one of these Stoney Sanctuaries.
>
> When you pass by one of these Stoney Sanctuaries, come inside and get some Rocket upgrades!

After the Green door
> This will be either a Homing Max Ammo or Homing Velocity upgrade. 
> This will be either a EMP Max Ammo or Nuke Max Ammo upgrade. 

> Some of the monks here dream of growing out of the [[Tau Caves]]. They are free to choose their own path. We help them on their way by putting 28 Rocket Radius upgrades at the top of this tower. Having a total of at least 5 Rocket Radius upgrades is useful for enlarging using [[Oak Tree]]s.
>
> However, to prove the commitment of monks, they must pass through the 3 6x6x6 mazes of this tower. This could take a LONG time.
>
> A much more well rounded way to get 5 Rocket Radius upgrades is to get the main treasure from 5 Stoney Sanctuaries such as this. Do you think there is a waypoint next to the yellow cube you are in now?

> [[Sagittarius A*]] told us a secret about WIG Flowers, which are of interest in the field of Inner Botany Theory.
>
> We wrote the message down and stored it in the tower above (after a 6x6x6 maze).

In the tower
> The message that [[Sagittarius A*]] delivered to our sanctuary:
>
> WIG Flowers have Pink Spheres on them and also usually contain a fragment of the Infinity Flower.
>
> WIG stands for "Williston In Gadget". That is, you enter the WIG Flower by shrinking and going into the Infinity Flower fragment. If you enter into Group #2 (I2), then you want to continue to shrink into Group #1 (I1) without passing by a Pink Sphere.  Then you activate a waypoint in a Williston City.  Then later, you can teleport back to that waypoint, and then travel nearby to find Pink Rings (which are probably inside a Pink Tree). When you touch the Pink Rings, you are teleported so that you are the size of the WIG Flower.
>
> There you have it: a "Williston In Gadget".
