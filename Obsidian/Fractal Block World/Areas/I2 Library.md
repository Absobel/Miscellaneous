> Message from [[SN1987A]]:
>
> The gems of Group #2 of the Infinity Flower are its [[Rainbow Flower]]s. These show what you can do with a powerful force of creation: you can create all the colors of the rainbow.
>
> These flowers are special because they contain LOTS of ammo. They also contain damage and experience multiplies (which stack). 
> 
> A Rainbow Flower has a Pink Ring, and also an I3 WIG Flower (which has a Pink Sphere). So you can "chain" these Rainbow Flowers together (to form "Rainbow Chains"). You do this by having the Pink Ring of one Rainbow Flower bring you to the Pink Sphere on the WIG Flower of a larger Rainbow Flower (one closer to the root of the chunk tree).
>
> The exact details of this chaining process are somewhat confidential, but most Inner Botanists know the procedure by heart. 
> 
> Needless to say, the chaining procedure consists of 3 parts:
>
> 1) Chaining each Rainbow Flower to the next.
>
> 2) Making sure the coarsest Rainbow Flower has a waypoint very close by (or very close by to the Pink Sphere that that flower teleports you to).
>
> 3) Making sure you can teleport to a waypoint somewhat close to a Pink Ring that will bring you to the Pink Sphere of the WIG flower on the finest Rainbow Flower (or else simply find a waypoint close to the finest Rainbow Flower).

> The center of the Tertiary part also has a chance (50%) of containing the Quaternary part of the Small Blue Flower.
> 
> If you reach the end of the Quaternary part, that is the end of the road. But you get tons of Rocket upgrades and also damage and XP multipliers.
