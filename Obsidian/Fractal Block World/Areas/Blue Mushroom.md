> Blue Mushrooms are challenging but are great places to improve your Railgun (the treasure has many Railgun upgrades). To get to the treasure, you have to shrink enough times and pass through the walls.  This takes some time. The walls of a Blue Mushroom are essentially a more challenging version of the Fanatic Lattice.
