Found in [[Mossy Air]]

Here you can find:
- Surface
	- [[Tall Rail Tower]]
	- [[Buttercup]]
	- [[Health Tower]]
	- [[Ying Flower]]
	- [[Jericho]]
	- [[Catacombs]]
	- [[Mossy Cellar]]
- Cavern:
	- [[Mossy Basement]] 
	- [[Steel Box]] (type 1?)
	- [[Health Tower]] (type 1)
	- [[Ying Flower]] (type 2)
	- [[Catacombs]] (type 3)

> There are caves below, and at the bottom a cavern.
>
> There are 3 types of caverns.
> Cavern type #1: lots of [[Health Tower]]s. 
> Cavern type #2: lots of [[Ying Flower]]s. 
> 
> Cavern type #3: the town of [[Jericho]] and [[Catacombs]].
>
> In Jericho you can buy markers for 4 gold a piece.
>
> Catacombs are challenging, but have significant dark hole upgrades, among other things.

> Within [[Mossy Air]], you are now standing on a Mossy Island. On the surfaces of these islands (at the size you are now) you can find [[Buttercup]]s as well as [[Tall Rail Tower|Rail Towers]].
>
> If you shrink one more time, you will find shafts in the ground that lead to a cave system below. Each island has different monsters and treasures.
>
> At the bottom of the cave system is a cavern. There are 3 types of these caverns. One of them contains the town of [[Jericho]]. In Jericho you can buy markers for 4 gold. Next to these Jericho towns you can find [[Catacombs]].
