> This is a Williston City! 
>
> The surrounding fields (separated by walls) are called Williston Fields.  To get back to the Main Branch, go into a Williston Field and shrink to enter a Bristol Town; Bristol Towns look like checkered cubes with the word "Bristol" on them.
>
> Most buildings in this city have Rocket Velocity upgrades. However, the taller buildings are more likely to have Railgun Max Ammo upgrades.
