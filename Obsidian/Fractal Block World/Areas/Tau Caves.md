> There are two places to go from here:
>
> 1) You can shrink into the [[Yellow Cube]]s that are in "[[Stoney Air]]" that surrounds you. Those Yellow Cubes are part of the [[Main Branch]].
> 
> 2) You can grow, using [[Oak Tree]]s. This is the standard way to grow in this place, however you can also grow using [[Hard Pink Tree]]s. Those Hard Pink Trees require you to be extremely powerful.
