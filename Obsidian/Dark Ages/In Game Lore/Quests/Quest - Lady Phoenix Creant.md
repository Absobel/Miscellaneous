> You hear something rustling in the trees. Birds fly away from the tree tops nearby. You run towards the area to investigate. As you get closer you can feel the ground trembling. Suddenly you see it, a [[Giant Ant]]! It burrows into the ground.

[[Shinewood Forest]] - 27 - Red Mantis:
> You hear a rather strange noise. It sounds like the noise of a [[Mantis|mantis]] but slightly different. You try to walk towards the source of the sound. As you get closer you can feel the ground trembling. Then you see it, a [[Red Mantis]]! It tears off the head of a normal [[Green Mantis|green mantis]] and then runs off into dense trees.
