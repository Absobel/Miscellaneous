[[Baltasar]]
"The Pentagram: Introduction"
> The [[Loures Castle Library|library]] was especially cold. A [[Grimoire of the Pentagram|dusty volume]] had just arrived. A volume excavated from [[House Macabre]] by an [[Aislings|Ailsing]] explorer, [[Bentic]]. It related the history of five previous occupants, the [[Scarlet Beetle]], the [[Kopfloser Reiter]], the [[Lich]], the [[Dark Cleric]], and the [[Nagetier]].
> One adventurer read in this tome of the ritual they had performed. It was a gruesome ritual. Yet, more gruesome when he realized some two hundreds years passed between the account of a man's life and account of the ritual.
> He understood the ritual could be repeated. With a sick sense of dread, yet an interminable curiosity he sought out companions that would be necessary to complete the ritual: A [[Monk|monk]], a [[Wizard|wizard]], a [[Priest|priest]], a [[Rogue|rogue]] and himself, a [[Warrior|warrior]]. Not any, but worldly-wise adventurers of at least three score insights.
> When he found the other four, he made them swear to secrecy. Then he told them of the tale and the ritual. He pointed out the passages in the tome.
> He asked for their agreement to a pact. They made a pact of five, one of each of the [[Aislings|Aisling]] classes.
> 
> "What happened next?"

> "Crazy tales"
> \[He narrows his gaze and then drops the discussion]
