The Sublime is a concept in mostly philosophy and aedthetic (at least that's the part i really find fascinating). It's the feeling that great, unknowable things evoke in you when you're not in enough danger that adrenaline takes over (though i'd argue it'd have to be actual physical survival instinct cause i'd be admiring the storm about to kill me if i had remotely the time to think about it). 
Examples are things like:
- Walking in the empty dark city at 4am
- Being at the brink of a precipice looking down
- The starry sky
It's exactly what represents the Vast in the Magnus Archives

[[Burke]]'s idea of the sublime i believe is that it's whatever creates ideas of terror and such, strong emotions, is a source of sublime. That it's definitely linked to fear. "obscurity, power, vacuity, darkness, solitude, silence and vastness". Evokes danger without being actually directly linked to danger
