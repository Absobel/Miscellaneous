# Rules summary

**Rolls:** d100, roll under the stat.

**Stats:** Physique, Mental, Social

**Level-ups** (x = level)
- +5 point à répartir stats. Cap = 65+5⌊x/2⌋
- +2 hp/mp par level. Total = 16+2x
- +1 spell/skill depending on what was before (lvl 3 competence, lvl 4 spell etc)

**Skills:** Passive, or active with a roll. No cost.
- En moyenne +10 à une compétence?
**Spells:** Cost mana or HP + a roll of the matching stat.
- 1-2 mana : d6, 3-4 : d10 ?

**Weapons** (short but deadly fights)

| Type   | Damage | To hit |
|--------|--------|--------|
| Light  | d6     | +0     |
| Medium | d8     | −5     |
| Heavy  | d10    | −15    |

**Combat** : 
- Initiative : d10 roll, mental stat for ties

**Rest** :
- Full hp/mp recovery

**Death**
- Swan Song
- Deep Consequence
- Unconscious for 2 rounds then (mental roll to help or other)
	- Deep consequence (but worse)
	- Death
